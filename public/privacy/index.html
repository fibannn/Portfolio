Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What this site collects

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts and no forms. If you email or phone Fiban Mubarak, that message and your contact details are used only to reply.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your light or dark theme choice and your cookie choice in your browser. These stay on your device. Optional analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) are only loaded if you press Accept in the cookie banner. If you press Reject, no analytics load. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Analytics

If accepted, Google Analytics records anonymised visit data and clicks on the contact links, so the owner can see which parts of the site are useful.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google servers when the page opens. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub are governed by those sites' own policies.

Your choices

You can clear stored data in your browser settings and ask for any email correspondence to be deleted by writing to fbnmubarak@gmail.com.
